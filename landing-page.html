Why Built for Oman

Generic marine apps don't understand Omani waters

🌊

Oman-Specific Thermoclines

Summer upwelling in Dhofar creates unique temperature layers that drive baitfish and predators—ignored by global models.

🐟

Regional Species Behavior

Kingfish and Hammour patterns in the Arabian Sea differ dramatically from Red Sea or Gulf populations. We model Omani ecosystems.

⚖️

Legal Compliance Built-In

Protected reserves like the Daymaniyat Islands require permits. BAHHAR geofences boundaries and reminds you of MOAF regulations.

How It Works

From conditions to catch in four steps

1

Set Your Location

Choose your port and target area—Muscat, Musandam, Salalah, or anywhere along Oman's 3,165km coastline

2

See Live Bite Score

ML engine analyzes SST, tide phase, bathymetry, and solunar cycles to predict 0–100 probability per species

3

Plan Your Trip

4-step wizard calculates fuel, departure time, and route based on your vessel and weather forecast

4

Log Your Catch

Record species, weight, GPS coords, and photos. Build your logbook and compare against predictions

Features

Everything you need for smarter fishing

Smart Trip Planner

Plan your entire fishing trip in minutes with our 4-step wizard that considers vessel type, fuel capacity, target species, and optimal departure windows.

  • Fuel consumption estimates based on distance and vessel
  • Tidal window recommendations for peak feeding
  • Weather-safe route planning with swell alerts
  • Save and share trip plans with crew

Interactive Marine Charts

Navigate Oman's waters with custom nautical charts showing hotspots, protected areas, and real-time probability markers.

  • Protected marine reserve boundaries with legal notices
  • Bathymetric depth contours and drop-offs
  • Color-coded probability markers (70+ = favorable)
  • Distance and bearing calculations from your port

Catch Log & Analytics

Document every catch with GPS coordinates, species data, and photos. Track your success rate against predictions.

  • Photo uploads with automatic compression
  • Species, weight, length, and gear tracking
  • Personal analytics and seasonal trends
  • Export logbook as PDF or CSV
