Why Oman-Specific

Generic apps don't understand Omani waters

BAHHAR is built from the ground up for the unique conditions of the Arabian Sea and Gulf of Oman.

🌊

Local Thermoclines

Summer upwelling in Dhofar creates unique temperature layers that drive baitfish populations—ignored by global models.

🐟

Regional Species

Kingfish and Hammour behavior in Arabian Sea waters differs from Red Sea populations. We model Omani ecosystems.

⚖️

Legal Compliance

Protected areas like Daymaniyat Islands require permits. BAHHAR geofences boundaries and shows regulations.
